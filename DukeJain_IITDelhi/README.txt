Bud-e (prototype)

Open index.html in any browser. No internet needed.

HOW TO OPEN
Double-click index.html. It works in current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox.
No server, installation or internet connection is needed.

HOW TO USE
- The panel on the left lists every screen, S0 to S14. Click any screen, or use "Next screen" (or the arrow keys).
  On a small screen the panel opens from the "Screens" button.
- Parent setup (S1-S4): sign up, child profile, nine permissions (consent is required), then the handover to Aarav.
- Aarav's app (S5-S13): chat with "My agent" in the centre; his areas, levels, tokens and wallet on the side.
  On the chat screens the request is pre-filled: press Send (or Enter). Tap the reply chips, or type your own words.
  You can also type your own requests: chores, any algebra equation, messages, purchases with a budget,
  a leave mail or essay, the Class 11 stream question. They go through the same rules.
- "Behind the scenes" (top bar, shortcut B) shows under each reply: child rating, task weight, gap and mode,
  then the rating update.
- "Faster response" appears while the agent is waiting on Aarav. It costs tokens and never raises the rating.
- "Presentation mode" (or H) hides the demo controls for recording; press H or "Demo controls" to bring them back.
- "Reset demo" starts over.

NOTES
- Every agent reply is scripted or rule-based. There are no real AI calls and no network requests.
- Persona: Aarav, 14, Class 9, Pune. 500 tokens a week, Rs 1,500 pocket money. Friend: Riya.
- The product name, tagline and level names are placeholders: change them at the top of assets/data.js.
- Anything outside the prototype shows "Not part of this prototype."
